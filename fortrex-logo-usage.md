# FORTREX FX Crest Usage

Use the regenerated crest as the primary Fortrex emblem while keeping the existing FORTREX FX wordmark unchanged.

| Context | Treatment | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Navbar | Small full-color crest, 36–40px | Use on obsidian or midnight backgrounds with clear space equal to one crest width. |
| Hero | Large full-color crest, 96–160px | Place over dark glass, chrome, or crystal surfaces; do not add competing copy inside the mark. |
| App icon / favicon | Crest-only crop | Use the shield and fortress silhouette at square aspect ratio; remove surrounding text. |
| Social avatar | Crest-only square | Use the gold-on-obsidian version with generous padding for circular crops. |

Do not stretch, flatten, recolor into neon-only treatments, or place the crest over busy charts. Gold remains the primary identity accent; cyan and violet are supporting refraction colors only. Official community URLs remain placeholders until supplied by the Fortrex team.
