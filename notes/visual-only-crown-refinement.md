# Visual-only crown refinement findings

The full desktop preview shows the crown clearly beneath the “Be early.” card, with a restrained gold/steel halo and no overlap with the card or centered hero copy. The mobile preview keeps the crown logo in the header and leaves the hero layout compact; the desktop-only focal crown remains hidden at the narrow breakpoint so it does not crowd the mobile CTA or sticky navigation. The visual direction remains black/obsidian first, with warm beveled gold and subtle spectral reflection rather than bright neon dominance.
